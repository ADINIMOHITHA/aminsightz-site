AM Insightz · Version 0.2 release candidate

Retainer Scope Kit

A practical, offline toolkit for freelance social media managers who need to assess extra client requests and prepare a clear scope conversation.

Pre-sale review — not yet availableWe are completing browser layout, download, and print-preview checks. Checkout is not live and no payment is being accepted.

What the current release candidate contains

01

Offline estimate calculator

Enter a retainer, baseline hours, extra hours, hourly rate, planning buffer, and currency label. It calculates an extra-work estimate and compares revenue per hour.

02

Editable reply builder

Creates a client-reply draft you can edit. The tool preserves your edits when recalculating and asks before replacing an edited reply.

03

Separate exports

The client-reply text excludes internal financial figures. A separate internal summary and print view contain the assumptions and revenue-per-hour figures.

04

Worksheets and examples

The ZIP includes editable scope and client communication templates, a compact free-sample checklist, a start guide, and a fictional walkthrough.

Who it is for

Independent social media managers who want to think through additional hours and prepare a written conversation before starting extra work.

What it does not do

It does not send messages, charge clients, convert currencies, save work automatically, or determine what a contract allows. Estimates depend on the values entered and exclude costs and taxes.

Testing status

The included automated suite passes 43 checks for calculations, validation, edited replies, export contents, and the walkthrough using Node and jsdom. That suite does not render the interface in a browser. Mobile layout, actual browser downloads, keyboard use, and print preview are still awaiting final acceptance, so this release candidate is not being sold.

Inputs remain in the open page and are not saved automatically. The tool makes no network requests and stores no data. Protect internal exports and use fictional information when testing.
